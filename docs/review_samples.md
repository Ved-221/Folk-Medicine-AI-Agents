## 3. Open Questions for Domain & Faculty Review
1. **Dataset Nature:** Is the current 500-record dataset a synthetic schema reference or vetted folk knowledge? Provenance tags (`classical` vs `folk`) are transparently attached to every recommendation.
2. **Missing Dosage Scaling:** When age-wise dosage is absent from oral folk sources, our policy displays *"Not specified in source, consult a practitioner"*. Should an expert-approved pediatric scaling rule (e.g. Young's rule) be supported as derived dosage?
3. **Red-Flag List Ownership:** The deterministic red-flag emergency symptoms list (`src/common/config.py`) currently screens for cardiac, respiratory, neurological, and hemorrhagic distress. Please review and suggest additional local vernacular red flags.
