### Executive Overview
- **Project:** Preserving Indigenous Folk Medicines — Research Honors Programme
- **Architecture:** Dual Deterministic Pipeline
  - **Agent 1:** Ingestion, Scaffolding Trimming, LLM-based Context Extraction with Verbatim Quote Validation, Semantic Indexing, Natural Language Query Parsing (Hinglish/Vernacular), Hybrid Retrieval.
  - **Agent 2:** Deterministic Safety & Red-Flag Gate, Pure Table Age-Banding Dosage Resolution, Constrained 6-Field Recommendation Formatter.
- **Core Principle:** *"Code governs decisions; LLMs only extract and rephrase grounded facts."*
- **Test Status:** 54/54 tests passing; Recall@3: 85.0%; Zero Hallucination Invariant Enforced.
